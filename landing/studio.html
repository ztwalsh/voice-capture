Push-to-talk dictation for macOS · v0.4.1

A key you hold. Text where your cursor is.

MoveThink is a menu-bar utility, not an app you open. Hold the hotkey in any text field, speak, let go. Transcription happens on your Mac, and every capture is appended to a Markdown file you can open anywhere.

Transforms · new in 0.2

Clean-up rules, written in plain language, run on the Mac.

A Transform is a short instruction applied to the transcript before it's inserted. General Clean-up is on by default. Add your own and switch between them.

  • Runs on Apple's on-device model. No key, no quota, no upload.
  • Built-ins can be switched off but never deleted.
  • The raw transcript is still what's saved to the file.
TransformsListCards
General Clean-upRemove filler words, fix punctuation and casing. Keep the meaning.
Standup bulletsTurn a ramble into three short bullets: did, doing, blocked.
Casual SlackLowercase, no trailing period, keep it short.
Commit messageImperative mood, 50-character summary line, body if needed.
Standup bullets · Turn what I said into at most three bullets under the headings did, doing and blocked. Drop anything that isn't one of those. Keep names and ticket numbers exactly as spoken.
The file

A local-first tool that hides its own storage is asking to be trusted. This one shows it.

Every capture is a heading and a paragraph in today's Markdown file. The window shows the real path, the ## markers stay visible, and you can open the folder in Finder, Obsidian, or a terminal.
