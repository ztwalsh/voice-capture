##A key, not an app.

There's no window to open and no Dock icon. Hold the hotkey in any text field on your Mac. A small capsule rises from the bottom of the screen with a live waveform so you know it's listening. Let go, and the text is at your caret before you've looked down.

Your focus never moves. The capsule is a panel that can't take it.

##Clean-up rules in plain language.

A Transform is a sentence or two describing what to do with what you said. General Clean-up is on by default. Add your own, such as "turn this into standup bullets" or "lowercase, no trailing period," and switch between them. They run on Apple's on-device model, so there's no key to paste and nothing to upload.
